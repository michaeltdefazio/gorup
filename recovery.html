Recovery: a general guide

A general timeline, not study results. Timing varies. Your own plan comes from Dr. Gorup’s team.

Neck recovery: a general guide

  1. Hospital

    Going home

    Most people go home the next day.

  2. Weeks 1 to 3

    Easing back in

    Light daily activity is fine.

    Most people are off pain medicine within 2 to 3 weeks.

  3. Weeks 3 to 6

    Back to work

    Many people return to work.

    Physical therapy often starts at 4 to 6 weeks, if prescribed.

  4. About 6 weeks

    Checking your healing

    Dr. Gorup’s team checks your healing and tells you when you can do more.

  5. Months 3 to 4

    Regular activities

    Most regular activities resume.

    Contact sports and climbing wait at least 3 months.

  6. Months 6 to 12

    The bones fuse

    Fusion can take 6 to 12 months.

    Visits and X-rays track your progress.

In shortMost people go home the next day. The bones can take 6 to 12 months to fuse.

Low back recovery: a general guide

  1. Hospital

    Your hospital stay

    Most people stay 2 to 4 days.

  2. Weeks 1 to 4

    Walking and healing

    Short walks as pain eases.

    The worst pain is usually over by about 4 weeks.

  3. Weeks 4 to 6

    Easing back to work

    Many people return to school or non-physical work.

  4. Months 1 to 3

    Building strength

    Keep lifting under about 10 to 15 pounds.

    Therapy may start from 6 weeks to 3 months.

  5. Months 3 to 6

    Getting stronger

    Activity builds as your surgeon clears it.

    Some pain can linger.

  6. Months 6 to 12

    The bones fuse

    Fusion can take 6 to 12 months.

    Visits and X-rays track your progress.

In shortMost people stay 2 to 4 days. The bones can take 6 to 12 months to fuse.
