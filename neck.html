About the procedure and the implant

About the procedure

In anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF), Dr. Gorup removes a damaged disc through the front of the neck. A spacer, bone graft and other implants support the area as the bones heal together.

About the implant material

CxHA combines PEEK, a durable polymer, with hydroxyapatite (HA), a mineral found in bone. HA runs throughout the material. Bone graft and your body’s healing are essential to fusion.

Could it be an option for you?

Your imaging, symptoms, bone health and goals guide the choice. Ask Dr. Gorup about your risks.

Who it may suit

Selected adults with degenerative disc disease at one neck level, after at least six weeks of nonsurgical treatment.

Risks include

Infection, bleeding, nerve or spinal cord injury, swallowing or voice problems, implant problems and failure to fuse. Symptoms may persist. More surgery may be needed.

In shortSurgery is one of several choices. Dr. Gorup can tell you which ones fit you.
